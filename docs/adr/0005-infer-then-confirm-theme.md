# Tema is inferred, then confirmed before the Dossiê

The Advogado does not start from a theme catalog, but the system also does not silently lock a recorte. After reading the Material do Caso, we propose a Tema from a short product list (a handful of recortes, even if only one has seed data). The Advogado confirms, picks another listed recorte, or chooses Outro and types a short name that becomes a new Tema. Pedido is extracted from the same text and edited on that confirmation step. No Dossiê is generated until Tema and Pedido are confirmed.

**Considered Options**: silent inference; Tema as a required first-screen field; one locked theme; infer-then-confirm against a short list plus named Outro — chosen.
