# A Dossiê is a snapshot, not an overwrite

Generating a Dossiê freezes the Precedentes that a Petição may cite. Regenerating produces a new Dossiê rather than silently replacing the old one. A Petição tied to the previous snapshot becomes stale and must be generated again; the MVP does not keep a version history of petitions. Overwriting the dossier in place would let the petition cite case law that is no longer in the dossier, which breaks the golden rule.
